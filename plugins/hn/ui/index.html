Flagged items first, then every dead item, whatever killed it. Killing hides an item and only you can bring it back; a dead item killed by flags can also be vouched for by members.
A dead ban hides everything the member posts from then on. A blocked member can still read but cannot post, vote or flag.
A blocked site also blocks its subdomains and kills their live stories. Removing the block brings those stories back.
Network keys come from the queue, never an address. A key hides the address it was made from.
